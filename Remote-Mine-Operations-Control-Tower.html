The Problem

Remote mines do not suffer from lack of software. They suffer from disconnected operations.

Critical operational risk is real, but it is scattered across CMMS, ERP, fleet, planning, SCADA, spreadsheets, and email — with no shared operating view.

Maintenance data is trapped in CMMS

Work orders show what failed, but not the production loss, cash impact, missing parts, or logistics risk.

Critical spares are managed too late

A missing component can stop production for days when lead times, air freight, winter roads, and sealift windows are not visible together.

Production plans and actuals drift apart

Mine plans, shift reports, fleet data, and plant throughput often live in different systems with different owners.

Logistics windows drive production risk

Winter road, barge, sealift, and air freight constraints are often managed in spreadsheets instead of linked to operational impact.

Camp, roster, and skill gaps affect output

Remote workforce availability, flights, rooms, certifications, and contractor mobilization directly affect execution.

Compliance obligations are not operationalized

Environmental, safety, permit, and Indigenous commitment obligations need owners, deadlines, evidence, and escalation.
